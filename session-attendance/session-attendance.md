# Session Attendance Tracking

**Status:** Available
**Category:** Session Management · Program Completion
**Pillar:** Training Management · Learner Experience
**Audience:** Training Providers · L&D / Enterprise (programs with face-to-face or live sessions)

---

## Summary

Session attendance tracking lets facilitators and admins record, manage, and export attendance directly in Promote — and optionally make attendance a mandatory requirement for program completion. Coaches can view the attendance of their learners, and learners can see their own status.

---

## The problem it solves

Programs that include live or face-to-face sessions need a way to record who was there — and ideally make that attendance mean something beyond a simple register. Without a built-in tool, attendance is tracked outside the platform in spreadsheets or separate systems, disconnected from the rest of the program data.

Attendance tracking in Promote closes that gap: records live alongside the program, completion criteria can include attendance, and exports are available when needed for compliance or reporting.

---

## What it enables

- **Track in real time** — mark attendance session by session, update it at any time, and keep a live record across all sessions in the program
- **Tie to completion** — sessions are often an important part of a Promote program. Include session attendance as a mandatory completion criterion — so completion reflects everything a learner was expected to do, not just the content they worked through
- **Export records** — export a full attendance list as CSV including name, email, username, attendance status, and session reference — ready for compliance records or reporting

---

## Attendance and program completion

Sessions are often an important part of a Promote program. You can now include session attendance as a mandatory completion criterion — so completion reflects everything a learner was expected to do, not just the content they worked through:

- Session attendance can be included as a mandatory completion criterion alongside tasks and quizzes
- When set as a completion criterion, attendance tracking is enabled automatically for that session and cannot be disabled while the requirement is active
- Works alongside other completion criteria — tasks, quizzes, and attendance together
- Removing attendance after a learner has already completed the program does not affect their completion status retroactively

---

## Who can do what

| Role | Capabilities |
|------|-------------|
| **Facilitators** | Mark/edit attendance from the session view. No learners are marked as attended by default — select each learner who was present and save. Can export. |
| **Admins** | Full attendance overview across all sessions from a dedicated Attendance tab. Same edit and export capabilities as facilitators. |
| **Coaches** | Read-only view of their learners' attendance status. Useful for follow-up with learners who missed a session. |
| **Learners** | Can see their own attendance status on the session page. No visibility into other learners' records. |

---

## Getting started

Attendance tracking is disabled by default and enabled per session:

1. Open a program and go to the **Builder**
2. Open the session you want to configure
3. Toggle on **Track attendance**
4. Optionally, add attendance as a completion criterion in the program's **Completion criteria** settings

---

## Positioning notes

- Lead with the **completion criterion angle** — this is what makes the feature genuinely useful vs a basic register. Recording who attended is table stakes; making it count toward completion is the differentiator.
- For **Training Providers**: frame around delivering credible, verifiable programs where attendance is part of the record — particularly relevant for compliance or certification programs.
- For **L&D / Enterprise**: frame around removing the spreadsheet — attendance lives in the platform alongside everything else, exports are available for audits, and coaches can follow up with anyone who missed a session.
- The **coach visibility** angle is worth mentioning for customers who use coaching heavily — coaches can proactively follow up without needing to ask admins for attendance data.
- The **CSV export** is quietly powerful for regulated industries — name, email, and attendance status in one file, ready for an audit trail.
- Attendance tracking is **optional and per session** — make clear it doesn't need to be turned on for every session, only where it's relevant.
