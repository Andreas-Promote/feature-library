New in Promote

Know who showed up. And make it count.

Attendance tracking built directly into your programs — mark, manage, and export records, and make showing up a requirement for program completion.
 

What it enables

✅

Track in real time

Mark attendance per session, update any time, and keep a live record across your program.

🎯

Tie to completion

Sessions are often an important part of a Promote program. Include attendance as a mandatory completion criterion — so completion reflects everything a learner was expected to do.

📤

Export records

Export as CSV for external systems, or PDF to share with anyone who doesn't have access to Promote.
 

Attendance that actually means something

Sessions are often an important part of a Promote program. You can now include session attendance as a mandatory completion criterion — so completion reflects everything a learner was expected to do, not just the content they worked through.

✓

Session attendance can be included as a mandatory completion criterion alongside tasks and quizzes

✓

Tracking is enabled automatically when set as a completion criterion

✓

Works alongside other completion criteria — tasks, quizzes, and attendance together

✓

Sessions can also be added to program goals — attendance then contributes to a learner's goal progress alongside their tasks
 

Who can do what

🎤 Facilitators

Mark and update attendance directly from the session. Changes can be made at any time.

Can edit · Can export

⚙️ Admins

Full overview across all sessions from a dedicated Attendance tab. Same edit and export capabilities.

Can edit · Can export · Full overview

👤 Coaches

See the attendance status of their learners — useful for following up with anyone who missed a session.

Read only

🎓 Learners

See their own attendance status on the session page. No visibility into other learners' records.

Own status only

⚡ One toggle to get started

Attendance tracking is disabled by default and enabled per session — go to the program Builder, open a session, and toggle on Track attendance. Set it as a completion criterion if you want attendance to count toward program completion.
