New in Promote

Meet your program's new AI assistants.

Create and manage AI assistants directly in Promote — no separate tool, no prompt engineering required, unless you want it.

What's an AI assistant? AI assistants live inside a dedicated tab in your programs, interacting directly with learners — answering questions, running practice scenarios, or reinforcing knowledge through quizzes. A guided form walks you through designing one, tailored to the type you choose.
 

Four types, one home

🧑‍🏫

Expert

Answers questions using your own training materials as its source.

e.g. FAQs about a compliance policy

🎭

Roleplay

Simulates real scenarios and gives immediate feedback on performance.

e.g. A difficult customer conversation

✅

Quiz

Asks questions, evaluates answers, and reinforces learning with instant feedback.

e.g. Testing recall after a module

⚙️

Generic

Write your own prompt from scratch, with no restrictions.

e.g. A fully custom use case
 

Build it your way

🧭

Guided setup

For Expert, Roleplay, and Quiz assistants, a guided form walks you through the relevant fields — no prompt engineering required.

🔓

Full control

Prefer to write your own prompt? Generic gives you a blank canvas with no restrictions.
 

Where this helps

💬

Give learners an always-available Expert assistant that answers questions about your training materials, day or night.
🎯

Let learners practise negotiation, customer service, or difficult conversations with a Roleplay assistant before doing it for real.
🧠

Reinforce retention with a Quiz assistant that checks understanding and explains what was missed.
🛠️

Build a fully custom assistant with the Generic type for a use case none of the guided forms quite cover.
