# AI Assistants in Promote

**Status:** Available (add-on, must be enabled per site)
**Category:** AI · Program Design
**Pillar:** Learner Experience · Program Design
**Audience:** Training Providers · L&D / Enterprise

---

## Summary

AI assistants can now be created and managed directly in Promote. A guided form walks admins through designing an assistant, with four types to choose from: Expert, Roleplay, Quiz, and Generic.

---

## Context: what's an AI assistant?

AI assistants live inside a dedicated tab in a Promote program and interact directly with learners — answering questions, running practice scenarios, or reinforcing knowledge through quizzes. Once created, they're added to programs the same way as before.

---

## The four types

| Type | What it does | Example use |
|------|--------------|-------------|
| **Expert** | Answers questions using your own training materials as its source. Accessible via a dedicated tab in the program. | FAQs about a compliance policy |
| **Roleplay** | An interactive character that simulates real scenarios and gives immediate feedback based on performance. | Practising a difficult customer conversation |
| **Quiz** | A quiz master that asks questions, evaluates answers, and reinforces learning with instant feedback. | Testing recall after a training module |
| **Generic** | Full control — write your own prompt from scratch, with no restrictions. | A fully custom assistant for a unique use case |

---

## Build it your way

- **Guided setup** — for Expert, Roleplay, and Quiz assistants, a guided form walks admins through the relevant fields for that type. No prompt engineering required — Promote's framework handles the underlying prompt construction.
- **Full control** — the Generic type gives admins a blank canvas to write their own prompt with no restrictions, for advanced or highly specific use cases.

---

## Where this helps

- An always-available Expert assistant answering questions about training materials, day or night
- A Roleplay assistant for practising negotiation, customer service, or difficult conversations before doing it for real
- A Quiz assistant reinforcing retention by checking understanding and explaining what was missed
- A fully custom Generic assistant for a use case the guided types don't quite cover

---

## Good to know

- **Requires enabling** — AI assistants must be enabled for a site before they can be used. Contact support@promoteint.com to get started.
- Once created, assistants are added to programs the same way as before — either assigned directly to a program's AI Assistants settings, or added as a resource to an assignment or task (reusable across programs).

---

## Positioning notes

- This is a bigger, more flagship feature than most in the library — give it room. The four types are the heart of the story and deserve real visual weight, not just a bullet list.
- **The guided-vs-full-control split is the strongest differentiator.** Most customers will use the guided forms (Expert, Roleplay, Quiz) and never need to think about prompt engineering — that's the headline value. Generic exists for power users, but shouldn't be the lead.
- For **Training Providers**: frame around offering more sophisticated, always-available support and practice opportunities as part of a premium program experience.
- For **L&D / Enterprise**: frame around scaling expert knowledge and practice opportunities without needing a live person on hand for every question or roleplay scenario.
- **The Remora migration note is CS context, not customer-facing copy.** Assistants previously built in Remora have been migrated automatically into Promote, and Remora's Text Bot management is retired. Useful for CS to know if an existing Remora customer asks, but this article is about introducing the value of what's available now — not the history of how it got here.
- Always note this is an **add-on that must be enabled** — direct to support@promoteint.com.
- Avoid overclaiming on AI differentiation — describe what the assistants do concretely (answer questions, simulate scenarios, quiz) rather than making broad claims about AI capability.
