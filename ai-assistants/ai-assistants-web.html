What's an AI assistant? AI assistants live inside a dedicated tab in your programs, interacting directly with learners — answering questions, running practice scenarios, or reinforcing knowledge through quizzes. A guided form walks you through designing one, tailored to the type you choose.

Four types, one home

🧑‍🏫

Expert

A knowledgeable assistant that answers questions using your own training materials as its source.

e.g. Answering FAQs about a compliance policy

🎭

Roleplay

An interactive character that simulates real scenarios and gives immediate feedback on performance.

e.g. Practising a difficult customer conversation

✅

Quiz

A quiz master that asks questions, evaluates answers, and reinforces learning with instant feedback.

e.g. Testing recall after a training module

⚙️

Generic

Full control for those who want it — write your own prompt from scratch, with no restrictions.

e.g. A fully custom assistant for a unique use case

Build it your way

Where this helps

💬

Give learners an always-available Expert assistant that answers questions about your training materials, day or night.

🎯

🧠
