What it means for your learners

One program, every language

Build your program once and enable translations for any of the languages available on your site. Members choose the language that works best for them — no duplicates to maintain, no one left out.

More than machine translation — a translation workflow built for L&D

🔍
Side-by-side review

Before going live, review translations in a side-by-side comparison view — original and translated content side by side, with language tabs to switch between all available translations.

✏️
Manual editing in the builder

Any translation can be refined directly in the program builder — click any content item to edit it. Your terminology, your tone, your call.

🔄
Re-translate on change

When you update content in the original language, Promote flags which translations are out of date. One click re-translates only what's changed — so translations never silently fall behind.

🎯
Translation context

Guide DeepL with industry terms, preferred tone, or role names that should stay untranslated. The result is a translation that sounds like your organisation — not a generic machine output.

How to set it up

1

Open the program settings

Go to the Settings tab of any program and find the Translations section.

2

Select your languages

Choose which languages to translate into from the languages enabled on your site. Promote translates the content automatically via DeepL.

3

Add translation context (recommended)

Provide guidance on industry terms, tone, or role names that should stay untranslated — this is what separates a good translation from a generic one.

e.g. "Leadership development program. Keep Coach and Sponsor untranslated."
4

Review before publishing

Use the side-by-side preview or edit directly in the builder. When you're happy, your program is ready for a multilingual audience.

The member experience

Language selector in the program

Members see a globe icon in the navigation bar and can choose their preferred language before starting — or switch at any time.

Full content in their language

Modules, tasks, goals, surveys, and quizzes all display in the chosen language. The original is always available as an option.

Transparent about translation

Optionally show a notice to members that content has been automatically translated — building trust with your audience.

No impact on participation

Language choice doesn't affect how members participate, complete tasks, or interact with coaches and facilitators.

🔀

Delivering the same program to separate cohorts in different languages?

Translation of Program Content creates an independent copy per language — giving each group their own dedicated space to interact in.
