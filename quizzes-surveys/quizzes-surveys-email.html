New in Promote

Check what learners know. Hear what they think.

Build quizzes and surveys right inside Promote — from writing the questions to following the results.

Two tools, one place. Quizzes check knowledge — questions are scored and learners get a result. Surveys gather opinions — evaluations and feedback forms where there are no right or wrong answers. Add either to a task in your program and follow the results as they come in.
 

What you can do

✅

Quizzes

Check what learners know

• Scored questions with a passing score

• Single and multiple-choice questions

• Feedback on each question, so learners see why an answer was right or wrong

• A summary of the result when they finish

💬

Surveys

Hear what learners think

• Evaluations and feedback forms with no right or wrong answers

• Rating and statement questions

• Learners can go back and see what they answered

• Facilitators see each learner's responses in the program view

Easy to build, flexible by design

Creating a quiz or survey is quick and straightforward. You have plenty of options for how to set it up, so you can shape the experience to fit your needs — and preview it as a learner will see it before it goes live.
 

Clear results for everyone

📊

Admins

Overview with detailed responses, a PDF report, and export.

🎤

Facilitators

Each learner's results, right in the program view.

🎓

Learners

Feedback per question and a clear result with score and pass or fail.
 

Where this helps

🧠

Run a knowledge check after a module and give learners instant feedback on what they got right and why.
🎯

Set a passing score to confirm learners have understood the essentials, for example in onboarding or compliance programs.
📝

Collect course evaluations and feedback to see how a program was received, and have the evidence ready for stakeholders.
🔄

Check in with learners during a program using a short survey, so facilitators can see how it's going.

🔗  Know where it's used. A Usage tab shows every program and task that uses a quiz or survey, so you can reuse them with confidence.

➕  Easy to add. Adding a quiz or survey to a task works just like adding any other task content.
