# Quizzes & Surveys

**Status:** Available
**Category:** Assessment · Feedback · Program Design
**Pillar:** Learner Experience · Program Design
**Audience:** Training Providers · L&D / Enterprise

---

## Summary

Quizzes & Surveys lets admins check knowledge and gather feedback inside Promote, from creating the questions to following the results. Quizzes are scored and give learners a result; surveys collect opinions with no right or wrong answers. Either can be added to a task in a program.

---

## What you can do

### Quizzes: check what learners know

- Scored questions with a passing score
- Single and multiple-choice questions
- Feedback on each question, so learners see why an answer was right or wrong
- A summary of the result when learners finish, with score and pass or fail

### Surveys: hear what learners think

- Evaluations and feedback forms, with no right or wrong answers
- Rating and statement questions
- Learners can go back and see what they answered
- Facilitators can see each learner's responses in the program view

---

## Easy to build, flexible by design

Creating a quiz or survey is quick and straightforward. There are plenty of options for how to set it up, so the experience can be shaped to fit the need, and it can be previewed as a learner will see it before it goes live.

*For reference (not for the overview copy):* a quiz or survey can have a start page and an end page, with pages in between that hold questions or just instructions and information. By default each page shows one question, which gives learners the best experience, and several questions can be shown on one page when it makes sense, for example a rating followed by a free-text "please explain why you rated it this way".

---

## Results for everyone

| Role | What they get |
|------|---------------|
| **Admins** | An overview for each quiz or survey with detailed responses, a PDF report, and export |
| **Facilitators** | Each learner's quiz and survey results in the program view |
| **Learners** | Quizzes: feedback per question and a results page with score and pass or fail. Surveys: review their own answers |

---

## Where this helps

- A knowledge check after a module, with instant feedback on what learners got right and why
- A passing score to confirm learners have understood the essentials, for example in onboarding or compliance programs
- Course evaluations and feedback forms, to see how a program was received and have evidence ready for stakeholders
- A short check-in survey during a program, so facilitators can see how it's going

---

## Good to know

- A **Usage** tab for each quiz and survey shows every program and task that uses it
- Adding a quiz or survey to a task works the same way as adding other task content

## Positioning notes

- **One article for now, two audiences.** Quizzes and Surveys are written as two self-contained halves so they can be split into separate articles as more capabilities are added to each. Lead with whichever fits the audience:
  - **Quizzes** resonate with teams who need to verify knowledge: onboarding, compliance, and certification-style programs. Lead with the passing score, per-question feedback and results.
  - **Surveys** resonate with training providers and L&D teams who need to show how a program was received. Lead with evaluations, rating questions and the evidence for stakeholders.
- **Lead with what it does, not where it came from.** This article should work for customers who have never used the previous tool.
- **CS context only (not customer-facing):** Quizzes & Surveys is the next generation of the earlier survey and quiz tool (Fetch) and replaces it without losing existing functionality. Existing quizzes, surveys and results move over automatically. Useful if an existing customer asks; keep it out of the customer-facing copy.
- **Do not reference yet:** a program-level results tab and creating a quiz or survey directly from a task are still in progress. Plans for future versions are not committed, so do not describe upcoming features.
- **Terminology.** The interface uses only **Quiz** and **Survey**. The word "questionnaire" caused confusion and is no longer used in the UI, so avoid it in customer-facing copy. Everything is a **page**: there is no separate "info page", just pages with or without questions. Question types are called **Single choice** and **Multiple choice**.
