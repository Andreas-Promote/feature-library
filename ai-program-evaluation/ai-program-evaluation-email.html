New in Promote · Beta

A second opinion on your program design.

Get an AI-powered assessment of what's working well and where your program could be stronger — aimed at making it more likely to lead to real behavioural change.

What does it look at? The AI Evaluation tab analyses how your program is structured, which types of tasks it uses, and how the content and any attached resources support learning — taking into account the learning tools available in Promote.
 

What you get

🎯

Overall impact

A short summary of the program's likely effectiveness for learners.

⚖️

Strengths and improvements

What works well and why, plus concrete recommendations.

🔎

The reasoning

The evidence behind each assessment, so you decide what to act on.
📚

Grounded in research, not guesswork

The evaluation draws on the High Performance Learning Journey (HPLJ) methodology — a research-based approach built on decades of research into what makes learning turn into real performance. It's the approach Promote itself is built around, now applied to your own programs.

Not a one-time check

Make changes based on the feedback — new tasks, adjusted content, a different structure — then re-generate the evaluation to see whether those changes made a difference.

Evaluate  →  Improve  →  Re-generate
 

Where this helps

🚀

Sense-check a program before it launches and catch opportunities while changes are still easy to make.
🔄

Take a fresh look at a program that has run before and see where it could be taken further.
🧭

Give newer program designers a structured starting point and a clear view of what good learning design looks like.

🧪  Guidance, not a verdict. This feature is in beta. The evaluation is intended to inform your decisions, and results may vary between evaluations of the same program.

🔑  Requires AI features. Available to admins who can manage a program, once AI features are enabled for your site. Contact your Promote administrator or Promote support to check.
