# AI Program Evaluation

**Status:** Available (Beta, requires AI features enabled per site)
**Category:** AI · Program Design
**Pillar:** Program Design
**Audience:** Training Providers · L&D / Enterprise (admins and program designers)

---

## Summary

The AI Evaluation tab gives admins an AI-powered assessment of a program's design, highlighting what is working well and where there are opportunities to improve. The aim is to help create programs that are more likely to lead to real behavioural change.

---

## What it looks at

The evaluation analyses how a program is structured, which types of tasks it uses, and how the content (including any attached resources) is designed to support learning, taking into account the learning tools available in Promote.

---

## What you get

- **Overall impact** — a short summary of the program's likely effectiveness for learners, and any structural factors that may be limiting its impact
- **Strengths and improvements** — what the program does well and why, alongside specific improvement opportunities, each with a concrete recommendation and a tag for the learning design dimension it relates to
- **Understand in more detail** — a deeper explanation of each learning design dimension, including how the program currently performs and the evidence behind the assessment, so admins can decide whether and how to act

---

## Grounded in research, not guesswork

The evaluation draws on the High Performance Learning Journey (HPLJ) methodology, a research-based approach built on decades of research into what makes learning turn into real performance. It's the approach Promote itself is built around, now applied to a customer's own programs.

---

## Not a one-time check

Admins can make changes based on the feedback (new tasks, adjusted content, a different structure) and click **Re-generate** to run a fresh evaluation of the program's current state and see whether the changes made a difference.

---

## How it works

1. Open a program and go to the **AI Evaluation** tab
2. Click **Generate evaluation**
3. Review the strengths and improvement opportunities
4. Update the program and **Re-generate** whenever a fresh look is wanted

---

## Where this helps

- Sense-checking a program before launch, while changes are still easy to make
- Taking a fresh look at a program that has run before
- Giving newer program designers a structured starting point and a clear view of what good learning design looks like

---

## Good to know

- **Beta** — the evaluation is guidance to inform decisions, not a definitive verdict. Results may vary between evaluations of the same program.
- **Requires AI features** — available to all admins who can manage a program, once AI features are enabled for the site (the same requirement as other AI features such as facilitator assistance). If the tab is missing, contact the Promote administrator or Promote support.
- **Built-in feedback** — thumbs up and thumbs down buttons let admins rate the evaluation, with an optional follow-up and comment. Feedback is used to improve the quality of the evaluation over time.

---

## Positioning notes

- This is a **beta** feature. Keep the tone measured: describe it as guidance and a second opinion, not as an authority on what makes a program good. Avoid claims that it guarantees better outcomes.
- Say what it actually does (assesses structure, task types and content, explains its reasoning, suggests improvements) rather than making broad claims about AI capability or uniqueness.
- **Frame the research claim carefully.** The HPLJ methodology is research-based and established; the AI evaluation is a beta feature that applies it and can vary between runs. Say the evaluation is *grounded in* or *draws on* the methodology. Avoid saying the evaluation itself is "proven" or validated, which would conflict with the "guidance, not a verdict" message.
- The **evaluate, improve, re-generate loop** is the strongest part of the story. It turns the evaluation into a working tool rather than a report.
- The **explanation of reasoning** (the "Understand in more detail" section and the methodology shown behind the question mark icon) is worth highlighting for sceptical customers: admins can see why a suggestion was made before acting on it.
- For **Training Providers**: frame around quality and consistency across the programs they deliver and sell on to their own customers.
- For **L&D / Enterprise**: frame around supporting program designers of varying experience, and a quick sense-check before launch.
- Because it is beta, **invite feedback** as part of the conversation. The thumbs up/down is a genuine input to how the feature develops.
