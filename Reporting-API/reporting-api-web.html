What data is available

Learner progress

Individual activity, task completion, and engagement across programs.

Program summary

Aggregated completion rates, task counts, and engagement per program.

Goals

Goal progress per learner and program — track whether objectives are being met.

Coaches & facilitators

Activity levels, engagement, and pending review tasks across your delivery team.

User qualifications

Qualification records including type, version, validity, and current status — ready for compliance reporting.

AI conversations

Interactions between learners and AI assistants — understand how AI is being used in your programs.

Comments & discussions

Individual comments and discussion activity across programs.

Users & permissions

User account data, sign-in information, and roles across the platform.
