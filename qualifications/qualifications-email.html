New in Promote

Qualifications

Track every certification, compliance requirement, and competency — automatically issued when learners complete programs.

The Qualifications feature gives you a single place to define, issue, and track the qualifications your organisation requires — with expiry tracking and notifications built in.
 

Three qualification types

🏅

Certification

e.g. Forklift licence, first aid certificate

Formal qualifications with expiry dates. Can be recorded for certifications earned outside Promote.
🛡️

Compliance

e.g. Annual IT security training

Mandatory recurring requirements. Always carries an expiry date so nothing slips through.
🧠

Competency

e.g. Leadership training

Skills and capabilities where expiry tracking is optional. Flexible for ongoing development goals.
 

What's included

⚡ Automatic issuance

Issued instantly when a learner meets program completion criteria.

🔔 Expiry tracking & alerts

Notifications for learners and admins before and on expiry.

📥 CSV import

Bulk-import historical records. New users created automatically.

✍️ Manual entry

Add individual records for certifications earned outside Promote.

🗂 Version history

Update qualifications over time while preserving each user's record.

📊 Built-in report

Export a full qualification report for audits and compliance reviews.
